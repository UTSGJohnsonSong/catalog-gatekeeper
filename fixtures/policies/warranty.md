# Warranty Policy

This policy belongs to Alder and Finch, a fictional store invented for this project.

## Standard warranty

Every product carries a 1 year limited warranty against defects in materials and workmanship, counted from the delivery date. The warranty covers the original purchaser only and needs proof of purchase.

## Bags and packs

Bags and backpacks carry a 5 year warranty on zippers, stitching and buckles. Normal wear such as fading or scuffing is not covered. Damage from overloading a bag beyond its stated capacity is not covered either.

## Drinkware

Insulated bottles and tumblers carry a 2 year warranty against loss of vacuum insulation. Dents, chipped paint and damage from a dishwasher are not covered. Lids and gaskets are covered for 90 days, and replacement lids can be bought separately.

## Outdoor gear

Tents, sleeping bags and other outdoor gear carry a 3 year warranty on seams and poles. Damage from fire, sharp objects or storage while wet is not covered. A repair may be offered in place of a replacement when that is the better outcome.

## Footwear warranty

Footwear carries a 1 year warranty against sole separation and seam failure. Normal outsole wear is not covered, and neither is damage from using the wrong size. Shoes must be cleaned before they are sent in for assessment.

## Making a claim

Warranty claims start from the order page with a photo of the defect. We reply within 5 business days with a repair, a replacement or store credit. The customer pays to ship the item to us, and we pay to ship the repaired or replacement item back.

## What the warranty excludes

The warranty does not cover misuse, accidents, unauthorized repairs or commercial use. Items bought from an unauthorized reseller are excluded. Nothing in this policy limits rights that local consumer law gives the customer.
