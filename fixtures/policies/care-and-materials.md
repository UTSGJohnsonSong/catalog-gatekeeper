# Care and Materials Guide

This guide belongs to Alder and Finch, a fictional store invented for this project.

## Washing knitwear

Wash wool and merino knitwear by hand in cold water with a mild detergent. Lay knitwear flat to dry and do not wring it. Never tumble dry wool, because heat causes felting and shrinkage.

## Cotton garments

Machine wash cotton garments cold with similar colours and tumble dry on low. Expect up to 3 percent shrinkage in the first wash. Iron on medium heat if needed, and avoid bleach on printed fabric.

## Leather care

Wipe leather with a damp cloth and let it dry away from direct heat. Apply a leather conditioner every 6 months to prevent cracking. Do not use household cleaners or solvents on leather.

## Insulated bottle care

Hand wash insulated bottles with warm soapy water and a bottle brush. Do not put insulated bottles in the dishwasher or the freezer. Air dry the bottle with the lid off to prevent odours.

## Waterproof fabric

Wash waterproof jackets with a technical cleaner and no fabric softener. Tumble dry on low for 20 minutes to reactivate the water-repellent finish. Reapply a durable water-repellent spray when water stops beading on the surface.

## Material claims

We use the word organic only for cotton certified to the Global Organic Textile Standard. Recycled polyester means at least half of the fibre weight comes from recycled sources. We do not make antimicrobial or health claims about any fabric.

## Storage

Store knitwear folded, not on hangers, with cedar blocks to deter moths. Store tents and sleeping bags dry and loosely packed. Keep leather goods out of plastic bags so the material can breathe.
