# Shipping and Delivery Policy

This policy belongs to Alder and Finch, a fictional store invented for this project.

## Delivery areas

We ship to every province and territory in Canada and to the 50 United States. We do not ship to post office boxes for parcels heavier than 5 kg. Orders to other countries are not available at this time.

## Delivery times

Standard delivery takes 3 to 7 business days in Canada and 5 to 10 business days to the United States. Express delivery takes 1 to 3 business days and is available only within Canada. Orders placed before 2 pm Eastern time leave the warehouse the same business day.

## Shipping costs

Standard shipping is free on Canadian orders above 75 dollars. Below that amount standard shipping costs a flat 8 dollars. Express shipping costs a flat 18 dollars, and shipments to the United States cost a flat 15 dollars regardless of order size.

## Tracking

A tracking number is sent by email when the parcel leaves the warehouse. Tracking may take 24 hours to update after the carrier scans the parcel. Parcels that show no movement for 10 business days are treated as lost.

## Lost parcels

If a parcel is lost in transit we send a free replacement or a full refund, whichever the customer prefers. Parcels marked delivered but not received need a carrier investigation first, which usually takes 5 business days.

## Customs and duties

Prices on orders shipped to the United States exclude import duties and taxes. The customer pays any duties charged by the carrier at delivery. Refused parcels are returned to us and refunded minus the shipping cost.

## Split shipments

Large orders may arrive in more than one parcel at no extra cost. Each parcel receives its own tracking number. Back-ordered items ship separately when they come into stock.
