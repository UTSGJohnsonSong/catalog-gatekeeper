# Sizing Guide

This guide belongs to Alder and Finch, a fictional store invented for this project.

## Apparel fit

Tops and jackets follow standard North American sizing from XS to XXL. Our regular fit leaves about 5 cm of ease at the chest. If a customer is between sizes, we suggest sizing up for layering and sizing down for a closer fit.

## How to measure

Measure the chest at the widest point with a soft tape and keep the tape level. Measure the waist at the natural waistline, above the hip bones. Compare the measurements to the chart on the product page, because fit varies by style.

## Footwear sizing

Footwear uses US sizing, and half sizes are available from 7 to 11. Measure the foot in the evening, when feet are largest. If a customer wears orthotics or wide socks, we suggest going up half a size.

## Kids sizing

Kids sizes follow age ranges, but height is the more reliable guide. Our kids line runs slightly large to leave room to grow. Check the height chart on the product page before ordering.

## Bag capacity

Bag capacity is stated in litres and measured with all pockets filled. A 20 litre pack suits daily use, and a 30 litre pack suits overnight trips. Stated capacity is a guide, and the usable volume depends on how the pack is loaded.

## Bottle volume

Bottle volume is stated in millilitres and measured to the brim. The usable volume is about 30 millilitres less, to leave room for the lid. A 750 millilitre bottle is the size most customers choose for daily use.
