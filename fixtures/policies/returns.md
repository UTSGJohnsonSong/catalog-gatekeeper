# Returns and Refunds Policy

This policy belongs to Alder and Finch, a fictional store invented for this project.

## Return window

Unworn and unused items may be returned within 30 days of the delivery date. Items must arrive with their original tags attached and in their original packaging. Requests made after the 30 day window are declined unless the item is faulty.

## How to start a return

Customers start a return from the order page in their account and receive a prepaid label by email. Each order may use one prepaid label. The cost of the prepaid label is deducted from the refund, except when the item is faulty or we shipped the wrong item.

## Refund timing

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 business days after the warehouse inspects the item. Store credit is issued within 1 business day if the customer chooses it instead. Original shipping charges are not refunded unless the item is faulty.

## Final sale items

Items marked final sale cannot be returned or exchanged. Gift cards, swimwear with a removed hygiene liner, and personalized goods are always final sale. Faulty final sale items are still covered by the warranty policy.

## Exchanges

Exchanges for a different size or colour are free once per order, as long as the replacement is in stock. If the replacement costs more, the customer pays the difference. If it costs less, the difference is refunded to the original payment method.

## Footwear returns

Shoes may be tried on indoors on a clean surface. Shoes with marks on the outsole count as worn and cannot be returned, so we ask customers to test the fit on carpet. The shoe box counts as original packaging and must be included.

## Damaged on arrival

Items that arrive damaged must be reported within 14 days of delivery with a photo of the damage. We replace the item or refund it in full, and the customer does not need to send a damaged item back unless we ask.
